Summary

Goal: This diploma thesis (Mendel University in Brno, 2022) examines the current state of Vietnam’s wood industry and its exports to Europe. It identifies factors affecting export performance, draws lessons from other exporting countries, and proposes solutions for exporting wood to the EU.

Methods: The author uses synthesis, analysis, comparison, document research and a logical method. These are applied to the Vietnamese export situation, EU market requirements, the impact of the EVFTA, and the models of China, Malaysia and Indonesia.

Main results: Vietnam’s wood export turnover reached USD 12.37 billion in 2020, up 16.2%. Vietnam’s exports to the EU amount to about USD 700-800 million annually against EU demand of up to USD 85 billion. The EU is Vietnam’s fourth largest market for wood commodities. Challenges include raw material shortages, costly FSC and CoC certification, traceability requirements under VPA/FLEGT, and limited resources of small and medium-sized enterprises. The EU pledged to eliminate 99.2% of tariff lines within seven years of EVFTA implementation.

Conclusions: Synchronized solutions are needed. The thesis proposes sourcing high-quality domestic raw materials, company standards, export promotion, risk management, enterprise linking and entry into EU distribution channels. Priorities are labor productivity, large-scale production and EVFTA compliance.
