Summary (≈185 words)

Goal: The thesis analyses how Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs) influence consumers’ purchase intentions in the Vietnamese food and beverage (F&B) market, answering two questions: how consumers are influenced by KOLs, and which KOL factors matter most. It then offers recommendations for F&B businesses.

Methods: The author combined 12 in-depth semi-structured interviews (conducted in Vietnamese on Zoom, February 2024) with an online questionnaire (n = 210, 1–15 February 2024) using 5-point Likert scales. Data were analysed in Microsoft Excel, including Chi-Square tests of independence (α = 0.05), alongside the author’s social media observation.

Main results: 81.4% of respondents had purchased or visited after seeing KOL reviews; Facebook (71.9%) and TikTok (58.1%) were the main platforms. Purchase behaviour showed no association with gender (p = 0.245222), but trend-following was associated with age (p = 0.000082). Fondness for KOLs (mean 4.13) and trustworthiness (4.01) scored highest; appearance (2.33) scored lowest.

Conclusions: KOLs significantly shape Vietnamese F&B consumption, and trustworthiness underpins the other factors. Limitations include an age-skewed sample (47.6% aged 18–24), small sample size, and translation issues.
