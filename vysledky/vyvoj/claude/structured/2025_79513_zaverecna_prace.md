Summary (≈185 words)

Goal: The thesis evaluates and compares the real-world performance of two photovoltaic (PV) power plants with different installed capacities and technical configurations, assessing operational parameters and key performance indicators (KPIs) over 2023 and 2024.

Methods: Monitoring data were extracted from the Solaredge platform for Power Plant A (Sentice; 39.8 kWp, 25° tilt, food-processing plant) and Power Plant B (Čebín; 139.44 kWp, 15° tilt, metal-processing plant). KPIs followed the ČSN EN IEC 61724-1 ed. 2 standard, including performance ratio, specific yield and capacity utilization factor. Measured output was compared with PVGIS simulations.

Main results: Plant A showed 74.84% utilization of own production, 41.35% grid dependency and a performance ratio of 0.615 (2023) and 0.622 (2024). Plant B showed 77.68% utilization, 53.32% grid dependency, and a performance ratio rising from 0.656 to 0.770. Specific yield: A 807.493 to 816.839; B 838.043 to 983.976 kWh/kWp. Plant A exported 25.16% of production; B 22.32%. Weekend overproduction occurred at B; an October 2024 drop occurred at A.

Conclusions: Both systems operate near their designed constraints. Plant A needs software-level refinements; Plant B would benefit from storage (10–15% of daily output), load shifting, soiling sensors, cleaning and hydrophobic coatings.
