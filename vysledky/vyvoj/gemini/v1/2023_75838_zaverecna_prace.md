**Goal**
This study analyzed the influence of Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs) on Vietnamese consumers' purchasing intentions within the food and beverage (F&B) sector to provide targeted recommendations for businesses.

**Methods**
The research employed a mixed-methods approach utilizing secondary literature and primary data collected in February 2024. Primary data were gathered through semi-structured, in-depth Zoom interviews with 12 Vietnamese consumers and an online questionnaire survey administered across social media platforms with 210 valid respondents. Statistical evaluations, including Microsoft Excel-based analyses and Chi-Square tests of independence, were utilized to assess variable relationships.

**Main Results**
Of the survey respondents, 81.4% reported purchasing products or visiting establishments after viewing KOL reviews. Facebook (71.9%) and TikTok (58.1%) emerged as the primary platforms for following KOLs. Chi-Square testing confirmed an association between trend consumption and age ($p = 0.000082$), with younger demographics (under 24) showing higher trend engagement (mean 3.49) compared to those above 44 (mean 2.19). Key factors influencing purchase intent included consumer fondness for KOLs (mean 4.13), trustworthiness (mean 4.01), highly authentic content (mean 3.84), and F&B expertise (mean 3.38), whereas appearance had minimal impact (mean 2.33).

**Conclusions**
The authors conclude that KOLs heavily influence Vietnamese F&B consumer behavior, with trustworthiness serving as a foundational element. Businesses are recommended to utilize high-performing platforms like Facebook and TikTok, carefully select trustworthy and authentic KOLs with relevant F&B expertise, avoid collaborators with private scandals, and leverage trend-based marketing primarily aimed at younger audiences.
