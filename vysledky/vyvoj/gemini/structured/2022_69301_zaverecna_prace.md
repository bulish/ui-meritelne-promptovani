### Goal

The study investigates the current state of Vietnam's wood industry and export activities, identifies objective and subjective factors influencing business and production performance, and draws lessons from international experiences to propose effective solutions for exporting Vietnamese wood products to Europe and other global markets.

### Methods

The research employs multiple analytical methods—including synthesis, analysis, comparison, and the identification of factors affecting wood exports—alongside document research on EU market rules and characteristics, market overview information, inheritance of relevant research topics, logical methods, and the study of global wood-exporting models from countries such as China, Malaysia, and Indonesia.

### Main Results

In 2020, Vietnam's wood and wood-product exports reached USD 12.37 billion, reflecting a 16.2% increase from the previous year, with total bilateral trade between Vietnam and the EU reaching USD 56.45 billion. Key advantages include abundant labor with costs at $1/3$ of Thailand and a young population where the labor force accounts for over 50%, alongside favorable trade frameworks like the EVFTA and VPA/FLEGT. Challenges involve a shortage of domestic raw materials, high costs for international certifications like FSC and CoC, strict EU environmental and technical regulations, and limited financial resources among small and medium-sized enterprises.

### Conclusions

To fully capture European market opportunities under the EVFTA and VPA/FLEGT agreements, Vietnam must transition its production mindset from small-scale to large-scale, improve labor productivity, establish domestic plantation forest material areas that meet market needs, enhance traceability and transparency, and utilize modern digital tools and distribution channels.
