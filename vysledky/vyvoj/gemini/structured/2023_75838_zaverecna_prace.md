### Goal

This thesis aims to analyze the influence of Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs) on consumers' purchasing intentions in the Food and Beverage (F&B) industry in Vietnam and to offer strategic recommendations for F&B businesses utilizing KOL marketing campaigns.

### Methods

The research combined qualitative and quantitative methods. Primary data were gathered through semi-structured in-depth interviews with 12 Vietnamese consumers and an online questionnaire survey completed by 210 respondents across Vietnam. Microsoft Excel was utilized to analyze the data, perform statistical calculations, create charts, and conduct Chi-Square tests of independence.

### Main results

The findings indicate that 81.4% of survey respondents purchased or experienced food and beverage products after viewing KOL recommendations. A Chi-Square test demonstrated a significant statistical association between age and consumption following popular social media trends ($p = 0.000082$), with younger demographics showing higher engagement. Furthermore, consumer fondness for KOLs (mean value 4.13) and KOL trustworthiness (mean value 4.01) emerged as highly rated factors influencing purchase intentions.

### Conclusions

KOLs significantly influence the consumption behavior of Vietnamese consumers in the F&B sector. While factors like trustworthiness, authentic content, and consumer fondness strongly drive purchase intentions, a KOL's appearance has minimal impact. Consequently, F&B businesses should carefully select trustworthy, relevant KOLs to optimize marketing strategies.
