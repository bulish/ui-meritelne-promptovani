Summary
This bachelor thesis investigates the influence of Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs) on food and beverage (F&B) consumer behavior and purchase intentions in Vietnam. Combining qualitative in-depth interviews (n=12) and a quantitative online questionnaire survey (n=210), the research evaluates how social media platforms and influencer characteristics shape consumer decisions.

The findings indicate that 81.4% of respondents have purchased F&B products or visited restaurants based on KOL recommendations. Facebook and TikTok emerge as the dominant platforms for following F&B KOLs in Vietnam. Among various influencer attributes, consumer fondness for KOLs, trustworthiness, and highly authentic content exert the strongest positive influence on purchase intentions. Conversely, personal appearance has minimal impact, while private life scandals can damage consumer trust. Furthermore, a Chi-Square test confirms a statistically significant association between age and following social media trends, showing that younger demographics are far more inclined to follow trends than older age groups. Ultimately, the study provides practical recommendations for F&B businesses to optimize influencer marketing strategies by selecting suitable platforms and authentic collaborators.
