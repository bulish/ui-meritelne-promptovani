This thesis investigates the export of wood and wood products from Vietnam to Europe, examining current market performance, influential economic factors, and international export models. It highlights that while Vietnam possesses a strong labor force, expanding plantation resources, and benefits significantly from trade agreements like the EU-Vietnam Free Trade Agreement (EVFTA) and the Voluntary Partnership Agreement (VPA/FLEGT), the wood industry still faces challenges. Key obstacles include a heavy reliance on imported raw materials, strict European environmental and product standards, a lack of transparent traceability records, and limited financial and negotiating resources among small and medium-sized enterprises.

To successfully expand market share in the European Union, the author proposes a series of strategic solutions. These include developing domestic sustainable high-quality timber resources under FSC certification, modernizing production and processing technology, establishing robust enterprise linkages, enhancing human resource training, and aggressively utilizing e-commerce and international trade promotion. Furthermore, enterprises must carefully navigate complex EU distribution channels and strictly adhere to stringent legal and origin criteria to maximize export efficiency.
