Goal: This thesis examines Vietnam’s wood industry and exports, focusing on the current situation, factors influencing export performance, international experience, and solutions for expanding wood exports to Europe, particularly the European Union (EU).

Methods: The study uses synthesis, analysis, comparison, document research, and logical methods. It reviews EU market characteristics, trade rules, export data, the effects of the EVFTA and VPA/FLEGT agreements, Vietnam’s export performance, and wood-export models from China, Malaysia, and Indonesia.

Main results: Vietnam’s wood and wood-products exports reached USD 8.52 billion in 2019, increasing 17.8% over the previous year. Total wood-sector export turnover reached USD 12.37 billion in 2020, up 16.2% from 2019. Vietnam exports wood products to more than 120 countries, but exports to Europe remain below potential: annual EU demand is reported at up to 85 billion US dollars, whereas Vietnam exports approximately 700–800 million US dollars annually. Key constraints include shortages of legal raw materials, limited forest certification, small-scale production, restricted financial resources, insufficient technology and human-resource quality, and strict EU requirements concerning traceability, legality, quality, packaging, and environmental protection.

Conclusions: Vietnam can expand its European wood exports by improving legality, sustainability, product quality, productivity, competitiveness, and compliance with EVFTA and VPA/FLEGT requirements. The authors recommend domestic resource development, company standardization, export promotion, enterprise cooperation, risk management, and improved access to EU distribution channels.
