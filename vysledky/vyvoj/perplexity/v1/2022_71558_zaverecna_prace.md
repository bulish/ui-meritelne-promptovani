Goal (25 words)
This thesis aimed to develop and establish European Forest Machine Operator (EFMO) occupational standards for tractors/skidders, forwarders, and harvesters, aligned with the European Qualification Framework (EQF).

Methods (35 words)
The study combined literature review and field research (interviews, meetings, focus groups); over 50 documents were reviewed and 30 stakeholder meetings held. National standards from the UK, Czech Republic, and Slovenia were mapped using content, comparative, thematic, and interpretive analyses.

Main results (90 words)
Five common competence areas were identified for each machine type: work planning and preparation, base-machine pre-use inspection and maintenance, safety checks, operations, and post-use inspection. Three EFMO qualifications were developed and rated at EQF level 4. Fourteen pilot tests were conducted, followed by 65 live candidate tests; by March 2022, ABA had certified 65 successful machine operators in Ireland. Harvester and forwarder qualifications were live tested and placed on the market, while the tractor/skidder qualification required further testing.

Conclusions (30 words)
The objectives were achieved, supporting the use of EFMO qualifications to improve safety, recognition, and worker mobility. The author endorses and recommends the new EFMO qualifications for use in operational forestry practice.
