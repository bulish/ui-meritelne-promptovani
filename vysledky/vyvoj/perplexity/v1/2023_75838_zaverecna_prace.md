Goal
To analyse the influence of Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs) on consumers’ purchasing intentions in Vietnam’s food and beverage (FB) industry and provide recommendations for businesses using KOLs in influencer marketing.

Methods
Mixed-methods study combining in-depth interviews (n = 12) and an online questionnaire survey (n = 210) with Vietnamese consumers, conducted in February 2024. Data were analysed using Microsoft Excel, including Chi-Square tests of independence.

Main results
Facebook (71.9%) and TikTok (58.1%) were the dominant platforms for following KOLs. A total of 81.4% of respondents reported purchasing or experiencing FB products after exposure to KOL reviews. Trustworthiness (mean = 4.01), consumers’ fondness for KOLs (mean = 4.13), and highly authentic content (mean = 3.84) were the strongest factors influencing purchase intention on a 5-point Likert scale. Expertise (mean = 3.38), follower interaction (mean = 3.34), and popularity (mean = 3.07) had moderate influence, while KOL appearance (mean = 2.33) had minimal impact. Consumption following popular trends was significantly associated with age (p = 0.000082), with the under-24 group showing the highest engagement (mean = 3.49).

Conclusions
KOLs significantly shape Vietnamese consumers’ FB purchasing behaviour, primarily through trustworthiness, authenticity, and emotional connection. Businesses should prioritize credible KOLs on Facebook and TikTok, ensure content authenticity, monitor follower interactions, avoid KOLs with private-life scandals, and tailor campaigns to younger demographics who are more trend-responsive.
