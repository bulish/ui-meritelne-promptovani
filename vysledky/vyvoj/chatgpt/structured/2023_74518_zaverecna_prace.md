1. Summary
Goal: The study examines the impact of cross-country banking sector development on innovation activities in selected EU countries over 2010 to 2020, testing the hypothesis that “Banking sector development is positively associated with innovation activities.” Methods: It uses secondary data for 24 EU countries as dynamic short panel data. Innovation is measured by the logarithm of patent applications (lpa), while banking sector development is represented by four indicators: Private credit by deposit money banks to GDP, Deposit money banks assets to GDP, Bank credit to bank deposits, and Private credit by deposit money banks and other financial institutions to GDP. The analysis applies pooled OLS, FEM, REM, and ultimately the two-step system GMM, with Arellano-Bond and Hansen tests. Main results: Models 2–5 show positive and highly significant banking-sector coefficients: 0.54, 0.98, 0.12, and 0.51, respectively. The Arellano-Bond AR(2) tests and Hansen tests indicate no serial autocorrelation and valid instruments. Conclusions: Banking sector development significantly supports innovation activities. The reported ranking of positive impacts is Deposit money banks assets to GDP, Private credit by deposit money banks to GDP, Private credit by deposit money banks and other financial institutions to GDP, and Bank credit to bank deposits.
