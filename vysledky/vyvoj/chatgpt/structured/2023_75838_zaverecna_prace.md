1. Summary
Goal: The thesis analyses the influence of Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs) on Vietnamese consumers’ purchase intentions in the Food & Beverage (F&B) field and identifies key KOL factors influencing purchasing decisions. Methods: The study used qualitative and quantitative methods, combining in-depth interviews with 12 Vietnamese consumers and an online questionnaire with 210 respondents. Interviews were semi-structured and conducted in Vietnamese; the survey contained 25 questions and measured eight KOL factors using a 1–5 Likert scale. Data were analysed in Microsoft Excel, including a Chi-Square test of independence, together with observation and content analysis. Main results: KOL trustworthiness was the strongest factor, with a mean value of 4.01. Authentic content also had strong influence (3.84), followed by consumers’ fondness for KOLs (4.13), KOL expertise (3.38), follower interaction (3.34), private-life scandals (3.15), popularity (3.07), and appearance (2.33). Consumption following social-media trends was higher among younger respondents. Conclusions: KOLs significantly influence Vietnamese F&B consumption behaviour, particularly through trustworthiness, authentic content, expertise, and fondness. However, the study acknowledges limitations concerning sample representativeness and language translation.
