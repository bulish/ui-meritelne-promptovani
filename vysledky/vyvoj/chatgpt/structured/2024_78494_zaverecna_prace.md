1. Summary
Goal: This study compares income inequality across all 27 EU member states from 2007 to 2023, using the Gini coefficient, Atkinson Index, and Generalized Entropy (GE) indices to identify trends, regional differences, and changes across income deciles. Methods: Disposable-income shares by decile were taken primarily from Eurostat (2024), “Distribution of income by quantiles”, with supplementary Croatian data for missing years. Calculations used the “ineq” library in R. Atkinson parameters ε = 0.5, 1 and 2 and GE parameters α = 0, 1 and 2 were applied.     2024_78494_zaverecna_prace Main results: EU-average inequality remained relatively stable, but national trends differed substantially. Romania’s Gini coefficient fell from 0.375 in 2007 to 0.305 in 2023, while Poland’s fell from 0.315 to 0.265; conversely, Sweden’s increased from 0.229 to 0.286 and Malta’s from 0.258 to 0.321.     2024_78494_zaverecna_prace Conclusions: Atkinson and GE indices revealed distributional differences overlooked by the Gini, supporting the use of complementary measures.     2024_78494_zaverecna_prace
