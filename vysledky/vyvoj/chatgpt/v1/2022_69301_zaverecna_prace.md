1. Summary
Goal:
To examine Vietnam’s wood industry and exports to Europe, identify factors affecting export performance, assess advantages and constraints, and use international experience to propose solutions for future exports.
Methods:
The thesis analyzes and compares documentary data on Vietnam’s wood exports, the EU market, export-promotion activities, and EVFTA-related conditions. It also reviews international wood-export models and evaluates export performance and influencing factors, with quantitative trends covering recent years, including 2016–2020.     2022_69301_zaverecna_prace
Main results:
Vietnam’s wood sector recorded export turnover of USD 12.37 billion, up 16.2% from the previous year. Bilateral Vietnam–EU commerce reached USD 56.45 billion in 2020, compared with USD 41.54 billion in 2019. However, despite EU annual demand for wood commodities of up to 85 billion US dollars, Vietnam exported only around 700-800 million US dollars to the EU annually. The wood industry’s industrial-output index increased by an average of 3.4 percent each year from 2016 to 2020. The EVFTA was expected to remove tariffs on 85.6 percent of tariff lines immediately and 99.2 percent within seven years.     2022_69301_zaverecna_prace     2022_69301_zaverecna_prace     2022_69301_zaverecna_prace
Conclusions:
The authors conclude that Vietnam has substantial EU export potential but remains insufficiently competitive. They recommend synchronized solutions emphasizing legal timber systems, worker productivity, domestic and plantation resources, larger-scale production, market research, competitiveness, and stronger integration into regional and global supply networks.     2022_69301_zaverecna_prace
