Methods: The study used a quantitative, structured descriptive survey of retail workers. Main results: 71.6% reported that new technology had changed their workload, while 82.9% expected substantial changes in retail operations. 66.9% agreed Industry 4.0 would reduce tasks performed by retail employees, and 69% perceived a threat to job security. 85.7% agreed that Industry 4.0 would result in fewer retail jobs. Although 84% reported receiving training, 72.7% said new skills and knowledge would be needed.
