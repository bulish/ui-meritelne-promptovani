Goal
The thesis analyses how Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs) influence Vietnamese consumers’ purchase intentions in the Food & Beverage (F&B) sector and identifies the KOL characteristics most relevant to purchasing decisions.     2023_75838_zaverecna_prace
Methods
The study combines qualitative and quantitative approaches: 12 in-depth interviews and an online questionnaire with 210 respondents in Vietnam, covering urban and rural areas. Data were analysed using Microsoft Excel, including Chi-Square tests of independence, alongside observation and content analysis.     2023_75838_zaverecna_prace
Main results
Among 210 respondents, 81.4% had purchased or experienced an F&B product, shop, or restaurant after seeing KOL reviews; gender was not significantly associated with this behaviour (p-value = 0.245222). Trustworthiness was a major factor (mean 4.01), while expertise had a relatively positive influence (mean 3.38). Highly authentic content also strongly influenced intentions (mean 3.84); 50% reported positive influence and 23.8% extreme influence. Popularity showed only moderate influence (mean 3.07). Fondness for KOLs was particularly influential (mean 4.13), whereas appearance was weak (mean 2.33). Follower interaction had a mean of 3.34, and private-life scandals a mean of 3.15.     2023_75838_zaverecna_prace     2023_75838_zaverecna_prace
Conclusions
The authors conclude that KOLs significantly influence Vietnamese F&B consumption behaviour, with trustworthiness and authentic content central to purchase intentions. They recommend that businesses use KOL marketing strategically, emphasizing credible, expert and authentic communication, while future research should use larger samples and more accurate research tools.     2023_75838_zaverecna_prace
