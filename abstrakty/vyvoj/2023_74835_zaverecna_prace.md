Recently, Ethiopia has faced instability due to the conflict between the central government and some groups of the Tigray regional government. This conflict caused a lot of damage in the country, especially in Tigray and Amhara Regions. Given this complex situation, most people moved within the same region or to other parts of the country to look for safe places, mostly in cities. A part of the population moved to refugee camps in different parts of the country and neighboring countries. It is expected that smartphones become a crucial tool these days for the displaced population. They use these devices for different reasons, such as to get information, for entertainment, to look for a better option, to communicate with family, friends, and other people, etc. The migrants who apply smartphones within these migrated communities also have reasons to use various mobile devices and applications. The main aim of the thesis is to conduct quantitative research on the reasons for using smartphones, mostly used mobile applications regarding their situation, and the ways of using mobile devices and applications. My survey target groups are 300 respondents between the 18+ age group among the migrated communities from Tigray and Amhara Regions. To perform the analysis, a Python programming language is implemented.
Machine learning models are trained to predict migrants’ reasons for smartphone usage and the most used applications.
