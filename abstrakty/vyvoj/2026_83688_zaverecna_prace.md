This bachelor’s thesis examines the relationship between early retirement age and the size of the shadow economy in selected Central European countries. The analysis focuses on Austria, Czechia, Germany, Hungary, Poland, Slovakia, and Slovenia over the period 2010–2024. The research is based on data from the World Bank and OECD estimates related to retirement age. The MIMIC model is applied to estimate the size of the shadow economy, and the analysis is conducted using Stata 19 SE. The results indicate that retirement age and early retirement conditions are not key determinants of the shadow economy in most countries analysed. Informal economic activity is primarily influenced by tax-related and institutional factors, particularly social security contributions and labour taxation.
