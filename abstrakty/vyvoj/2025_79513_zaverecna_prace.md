This diploma thesis focuses on the operational evaluation of two photovoltaic power plants located in the South Moravian Region of the Czech Republic. The primary objective is to analyze and compare the crucial operational parameters of two photovoltaic systems over the years 2023 and 2024, with emphasis on energy yield, efficiency, and system utilization under real-world operating conditions. The selected systems, referred to as Power Plant A and Power Plant B, differ in installed capacity, panel configuration, consumption profiles, and the industrial environments to which the generated energy is delivered. This allowed for a comparative analysis within the context of two distinct industrial applications. Measured and monitored data were collected via the SolarEdge monitoring platform, providing detailed insights into system behavior and energy flow. The evaluation applies selected performance metrics aligned with the ČSN EN IEC 61724-1 ed. 2 standard, including performance ratio and capacity utilization factor, enabling consistent and objective performance assessment. In addition, data simulated using PVGIS was used to provide an overall comparison of both already observed and simulated data. The overall findings indicate that while Power Plant A maintained stable performance aligned with its consumption profile, Power Plant B demonstrated notable improvements during the monitored period. Identified inefficiencies related to environmental conditions and system mismatches were addressed through targeted recommendations, such as load shifting, mitigation of panel soiling, and other corrective measures. Furthermore, potential system enhancements were proposed, including the integration of battery storage for Power Plant B, which exhibited significant grid feed-in surpluses. The findings highlight the importance of tailored performance monitoring and demonstrate the broader applicability of the proposed evaluation approach for industrial- scale photovoltaic systems aiming to improve energy efficiency and grid autonomy. The applied methodology also enables a structured assessment of photovoltaic performance under real-world conditions and supports analysis across diverse operational scenarios.
