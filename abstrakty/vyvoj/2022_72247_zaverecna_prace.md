This bachelor thesis focuses on the rights of Palestinian minors from the West Bank in relation with Israeli authorities based on International law. It will contrast the legal obligations contracted by the state of Israel through ratification of interna- tional texts with the reality on the ground, from the Second Intifada onwards.
The work will first be placed into a socio-political context before adressing all the major international legal texts ratified by the state of Israel related to the welfare of minors. It will then describe the concrete situation on the ground concerning Pales- tinian minors in the West Bank when being in contact with Israeli authorites. It will finally go through military amendments from the past decade targeting the improve- ment of the minor’s welfare, as well as criticism which has been made about it.
