In recent years, the influence of Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs) on consumer pur- chase intentions in Vietnam, particularly in the Food and Beverage (F&B) field, has become an explosive phenomenon. As a result, influencer marketing has become more popular among F&B brands and businesses. This thesis delves into the con- sumption situation of Vietnamese consumers after exposure to marketing activi- ties of KOLs. The objective of this thesis is to analyse the influence of KOLs on con- sumer purchase intentions and provide practical recommendations for F&B busi- nesses in utilizing KOL marketing effectively. Additionally, key factors of KOLs are identified to help in improving shortcomings in KOL marketing campaigns of busi- nesses. Both quantitative and qualitative research methods are employed in this thesis. Data is collected from consumers across Vietnam through in-depth inter- views (n = 12) and online surveys (n = 210).
