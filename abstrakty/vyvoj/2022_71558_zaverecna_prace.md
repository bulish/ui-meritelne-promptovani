Associated with an increasing demand for skilled forest machine operators across borders is a reported EU-wide increase in harvesting machinery accidents and fatalities. Additionally, forest machine operators face barriers to employment due to non-recognition of their qualifications in other European countries. This is exacerbated due to different national systems of vocational training. This study evaluated national qualifications and developed harmonised Pan-European qualifications. These were pilot tested and subsequently released on to the market place providing an innovative and concrete solution to the issue.
