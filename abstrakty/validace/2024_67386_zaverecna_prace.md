This study assesses the social, economic, and environmental effects of harvesting timber on residents around the Kambui Hills Forest Reserve, Kenema district in Sierra Leone. The methodology is a hybrid of regression analysis and descriptive statistical analysis. The regression analysis uses a time series data set (2010-2021) from United Nations data bases to specifically identify timber harvesting impacts of identified variables on the environment, the economy and the social fabrics of Sierra Leone. The descriptive statistical analysis uses primary data collected from the survey area. Data were collected in a controlled survey from four communities, using a questionnaire formulated according to guidelines recommended by Babbie and Mouton (2010). The Reliability test shows a 0.68 internal consistency among the latent variables (Cronbach alpha test). Results from the primary survey using confirmatory factor analysis confirmed that the factors that influence environmental impacts from timber harvesting have a 1.08 Eigen values and explained 12 % of the total variance. Similarly, the economic impacts from timber harvesting have 1.08 Eigen values and explained 20 % of the total variance. In the same vein, the social impacts from timber harvesting in the study area have 1.63 Eigen values and explain 19 % of the total variance. Results from regression analysis confirmed that R2 or the coefficient of determination is 0.5734, which shows that 57 % of the dependent variable is significantly explained by the independent variables in the model representing environmental impacts. Similarly, the economic impact shows an R2 of 0.8596, which indicates that 86 % of the dependent variable is explained by the independent variables in the model. For the social impacts, R2 shows an R-square of 0.9667. This is statistically significant as 97 % of the dependent variable is explained by the independent variables in the model. Thus, both models confirmed the socio-economic effects of harvesting timber and its environmental impact on the Kambui Hills Forest Reserve in Kenema district, Sierra Leone. This study will conclude with recommendations to the relevant stakeholders in Sierra Leone.
