This study examines the working conditions and labor rights of garment workers in the Ready- Made Garment (RMG) sector, with a specific focus on factories located in Gazipur district. The research adopts a mixed-method approach, combining quantitative survey data and qualitative insights from worker experiences. The findings reveal that while the RMG sector significantly contributes to economic growth and employment generation, workers face numerous challenges, including long working hours, low wages, inadequate workplace safety, and limited labor rights protection. Gender-based vulnerabilities further intensify these issues, particularly for female workers. The study also identifies gaps between existing labor laws and their practical implementation. Based on the findings, the research proposes practical recommendations to improve working conditions, strengthen labor rights enforcement, and promote worker well-being. Overall, the study highlights the need for collaborative efforts among stakeholders to ensure sustainable and ethical development within the garment industry.
