This study uses a multi-logit model to analyze the factors affecting rural residents' choice of insurance contribution brackets based on data from the 2019 Tengzhou city field survey. It was found that gender, age, marital status, education, health, number of children, annual per capita household income, total household assets, understanding of pension subsidies, understanding of the procedure for changing contribution brackets, satisfaction with the processing of insurance, and satisfaction with the basic pension payment influenced the choice of contribution brackets. Based on this, the thesis proposes corresponding policy recommendations to promote the process of equalization of urban and rural old- age insurance services.
