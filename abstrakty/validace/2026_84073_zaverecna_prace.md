This thesis focuses on analyzing the influence of trust in financial institutions on the structure of household asset allocation. The study provides a theoretical justification and empirical verification of the role of trust as a factor determining households’ propensity to invest capital and accumulate formal savings. The methodological framework utilizes cross-sectional data and econometric modeling via the Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) method, implemented using Gretl software. The findings in- dicate that institutional trust is a crucial determinant in the household financial decision-making process. This work demonstrates the importance of institutional development for fostering a sustainable investment environment and identifies per- spectives for future research.
