This research delves into the complexities within Sri Lanka's bio-based industries, examining how policy frameworks, technological innovations, stakeholder en- gagement, and market dynamics interconnect. It sheds light on the significant con- sequences of bio-blunders and policy gaps, aiming to comprehend the root causes of the economic crisis impacting the agricultural sector. The study employs a robust methodology, blending conceptual frameworks, correlational analyses, and detailed surveys to unveil the multifaceted challenges these industries face.
Chapter-wise exploration reveals layers of industry intricacies, starting from dissecting its landscape to identifying the crisis symptoms. Methodological chapters detail frameworks, hypotheses, and research designs, culminating in meticulous data analysis utilizing statistical tools and comparative methods. The findings em- phasize the pivotal role of various elements in driving sustainability. Technological advancements emerge as a linchpin, exerting a profound influence on industry sustainability. While policy frameworks wield a moderate impact, stakeholder en- gagement, and market access are pivotal for sustainable growth. The study proposes actionable recommendations directed at stakeholders and policymakers. These suggestions advocate for targeted investments in technology, strengthened collabo- ration among stakeholders, refined policy structures, and fostering a culture of con- tinuous improvement. Implementing these suggestions could trigger a transforma- tive shift toward sustainability in Sri Lanka's bio-based industries.
Furthermore, the research spotlights promising avenues for future explora- tion. It invites scholars and industry experts to delve deeper into policy analyses, technological assessments, stakeholder dynamics, market access strategies, em- ployee satisfaction correlations, and interdisciplinary approaches. These areas pro- mise to enrich discussions on sustainable development within Sri Lanka's bio-based industries. In essence, this thesis offers a comprehensive understanding of challen- ges, impactful relationships, and strategic recommendations crucial for steering Sri Lanka's bio-based industries toward sustainability. Its findings contribute not only to academic discourse but also offer practical insights capable of reshaping industry practices, fostering innovation, and propelling the nation toward a more sustainable future.
