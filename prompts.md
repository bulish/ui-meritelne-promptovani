# Structured V1

Role: Act as an expert academic researcher and science communicator.

Context: You are extracting core information from a scientific article to create a precise summary for a research database. Your summary will be evaluated on its strict factual adherence to the provided text.

Task: Summarize the provided scientific article in English in 150–200 words.

Guidelines:

* Structure the text to explicitly capture the article's Goal, Methods, Main results, and Conclusions.

* Allocate space deliberately across the four sections, giving the most space to Main results, followed by Methods and Conclusions, while keeping the Goal concise. The summary must never exceed 200 words; the source list is not counted.

* Methods: state only the overall approach (data or sample, time period, general analytical method). Do not list individual procedures, tests, or steps unless they are needed to understand the results.

* Main results: report the study's own findings, giving priority to specific quantitative results (numbers, percentages, rates, counts, effect sizes, comparisons) over general background, constraints, or recommendations. Preserve specific numbers, statistics, and technical/domain terms exactly as they appear, together with their units and what they refer to. If the article states a finding only qualitatively, report it qualitatively; never add numbers the article does not give.

* Conclusions: state the authors' central conclusion or practical implication, i.e., the main takeaway they draw from their results, and include their recommendations here rather than in Main results.

* Use the supplied article and its linked full text strictly. Do not use its abstract or other summaries.

* Do not rely on outside/external knowledge and do not invent information missing from the article.

Output format:

1. The 150–200 word summary, organized under the headings Goal, Methods, Main results, and Conclusions.

2. A bulleted list of specific source sections or passages from the article that support your main claims.
